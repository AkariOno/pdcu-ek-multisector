01 · Read the indices

In pi[n, i], what does each index identify?

Each row is one importer's allocation of expenditure across exporters. Rows sum to one; there is no industry axis in this baseline.

02 · Match code to an equation

Select the block that computes the denominator of the trade-share equation in log space.

row_logsumexp uses NumPy row-max shifting and sums over exporter columns within each importer row. Exponentiating log_denominator gives the trade-share denominator.

03 · Close the economy

Which equation determines relative wages? Explain the sales/income update, fixed damping, and country-0 normalization.

Exporter sales equal labor income: sum_n pi[n,i] w[n]L[n] = w[i]L[i]. The iteration multiplies each wage by (sales/income)^alpha, then normalizes all wages by country 0. Before normalization, q>1 raises an exporter's wage and q<1 lowers it. alpha=0.2 applies a partial log adjustment. A numeraire removes nominal scale indeterminacy; convergence checks all N residuals.

04 · Change the numeraire

If all wages are rescaled by a common positive factor, what happens?

The price index is homogeneous of degree one in wages. Common nominal scaling cancels from expenditure shares and w/P. Both verification routes must use the same wage normalization.

05 · Supply exact hats

Which information enters the exact-hat equilibrium equations for this trade-cost shock?

Baseline shares summarize technology and delivered-cost competitiveness. Baseline incomes supply spending weights. The equations do not need the counterfactual levels solution. The API also retains primitives to validate shocked iceberg costs.

06 · Explain two routes

Why should E1/E0 equal the exact-hat result, and what would agreement alone fail to prove?

Divide the counterfactual equations by their baseline counterparts with technology, labor, theta, and gamma fixed. Baseline shares then replace levels competitiveness terms. 07 · Diagnose a false pass

All comparison errors are 1e−13 and wage changes are tiny, but the hat route reaches max_iterations with a full residual of 2e−4. What is the verdict?

Fail: small steps can reflect strong damping or stagnation. The iteration must reach its full residual target (default 1e−13), and verification requires all routes to converge, residual norms below 1e−11, and both error measures below 1e−9. An iteration-limit status is not convergence.

08 · Interpret the shock

Trace the 10% bilateral trade-cost cut through shares, wages, prices, and real income. Why can country 2 also change?

Lower bilateral delivered costs initially increase the affected exporters' competitiveness. Expenditure shifts change sales and relative wages; those wage changes feed back into every importer's prices and shares. Welfare is the wage change divided by the price change. Country 2 participates in the same market-clearing system, so its outcomes can change even though its trade costs do not. Direction and magnitude should be read from the equilibrium, not assumed for every country.
