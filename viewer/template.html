The experiment

A 10% bilateral trade-cost cut between countries 0 and 1. Technology and labor stay fixed.

Compute changes through two full equilibria and independently through exact-hat algebra.

Acceptance: all three solves converge; every absolute and relative comparison error is below 10⁻⁹.

The transparent wage iteration · NumPy only

q[i] = sales[i] / income[i]
log_w_next[i] = log_w[i] + 0.2 * log(q[i])
log_w_next[:] -= log_w_next[0]

Start with unit wages. Evaluate shares, incomes, and sales; check every country's residual; then update all wages simultaneously and normalize. Repeat with fixed damping. The same rule applies to wage hats.

Before normalization, q > 1 raises a wage; q < 1 lowers it. Defaults: damping 0.2, residual target 1e-13, and 10,000 updates. Small wage changes alone do not imply convergence.
