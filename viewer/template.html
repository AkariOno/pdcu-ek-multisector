The experiment

A 10% bilateral trade-cost cut in industry 0 only between countries 0 and 1. Technology, labor, demand shares, elasticities and gamma stay fixed.

01 / Plan

What is being solved?

One country wage clears aggregate income across all industries. Industry trade does not need to balance separately.

n · importer / axis 0i · exporter / axis 1j · industry / axis 2
Conditional industry trade shares

B[n,i,j] = T[i,j] (w[i] d[n,i,j])^(−theta[j])
S[n,j] = sum_i B[n,i,j]
pi[n,i,j] = B[n,i,j] / S[n,j]

Industry prices

P[n,j] = gamma[j] S[n,j]^(−1/theta[j])

Normalized utility, cost of living & welfare

U[n] = product_j (c[n,j]/alpha[n,j])^alpha[n,j]
C[n] = product_j P[n,j]^alpha[n,j]
real_wage[n] = w[n] / C[n]

No expenditure-share constants enter C. The superseded utility convention rescales welfare levels, but fixed preferences imply the same welfare ratios.

Expenditure & market clearing

income[n] = w[n] L[n]
X[n,j] = alpha[n,j] income[n]
sales[i] = sum_n sum_j pi[n,i,j] X[n,j]
sales[i] = income[i]

Primitives → full solution
T: (N,J), L: (N,), d: (N,N,J), theta and gamma: (J,), alpha: (N,J). Positive alpha rows sum to one. Costs ≥ 1; domestic costs = 1.
Baseline statistics + shock → exact hats
pi0[n,i,j], income0[n], theta[j], alpha[n,j], and d_hat[n,i,j]. The independent route never calls levels competitiveness.

a[n,i,j] = pi0[n,i,j] (w_hat[i] d_hat[n,i,j])^(−theta[j])
A[n,j] = sum_i a[n,i,j]
pi1[n,i,j] = a[n,i,j] / A[n,j]
P_hat[n,j] = A[n,j]^(−1/theta[j])
C_hat[n] = product_j P_hat[n,j]^alpha[n,j]
income1[n] = income0[n] w_hat[n]
X1[n,j] = alpha[n,j] income1[n]
real_wage_hat[n] = w_hat[n] / C_hat[n]

Unknowns & numeraire
N wages with N−1 independent relative values. w[0] = w_hat[0] = 1 after every update.
Labor and closure
Full employment; labor mobile between domestic industries, immobile internationally. Expenditure equals labor income; aggregate trade is balanced.
Checked after solving
Every country's normalized residual is below 1e-13 by default; numerical objects are finite and positive. Verification also enforces the original 1e-11 acceptance bound.
Transparent NumPy wage iteration

q[i] = sales[i] / income[i]
log_w_next[i] = log_w[i] + damping * log(q[i])
log_w_next[:] -= log_w_next[0]

Start from unit wages. Evaluate → check all country residuals → update simultaneously → normalize → reevaluate. Fixed damping 0.2; at most 10,000 updates. Damping is distinct from expenditure shares alpha.

No optimizer fallback, line search, or success based on small wage changes. Explicit failures retain the last evaluated state.
